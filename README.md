**Current code-research starting points:** Neil Strickland's Owl pants geometry; homotopy.io's compositional geometry and movies; Andrew Stacey's TQFT diagrams; MetaKnight and KLO for Kirby-move algorithms and interaction. The survey distinguishes inspected source, documented features, licensing questions, and untested adaptation ideas. No third-party implementation has been vendored.

**Scope:** Keep an abstract topological object and its dimensional data separate from its mesh, camera, diagram projection, and animation. A convincing 3D rendering is not by itself a proof of a 4-dimensional handle move.

**Sources and permissions:** Write original mathematical notes. Link to books, papers and lectures rather than uploading publisher PDFs, copied figures, or third-party videos without permission. Preserve explicit author credit and provenance.
